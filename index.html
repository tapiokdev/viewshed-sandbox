help & verification tips

Modes (top of the panel). observer — click a cell to see what it sees. drone A→B — click A then B; the drone flies straight at its set height above local ground and the green/red overlay follows it, routing around terrain it can't clear under the flight ceiling. exposure — click a target to see everywhere it could be seen from. Switching modes keeps the marked point and reinterprets its role. Drag the map in any mode to pilot the point live.

The altimeter beside the map is the elevation ramp and, in drone mode, the flight ceiling. Drag it to set the ceiling; the purple band is the heights the drone can't clear and a tick marks the current eye altitude. Hover it to preview those walls on the map. While a route is being planned the walls also outline themselves in purple, and sensor coverage shows in pale orange, on their own — the viewshed overlay arrives with the launch. On narrow screens the altimeter becomes a horizontal Flight ceiling slider under the map.

Flight. The strip under the map holds pause/resume, replay, a progress bar and a live countdown; a chip by the status line reads planning / route pending / flying / paused / landed. If no route fits under the ceiling the flight goes route pending: A and B stay joined by a dashed red line, every change retries the plan, and it takes off by itself ~3 s after a route appears (auto-resume; otherwise press replay flight). A mid-flight change that walls off B freezes the drone in place — coverage intact — until a route reappears. Landed flights re-fly after 5 s (auto-replay), so a shared link loops on its own; the speed slider changes playback live, and a manual pause always sticks.

Sensors. With a mouse, hold the right button to place a mast — it follows the cursor and commits on release; right-drag a mast to move it, right-click to remove it. On a touch screen, switch the tool above the map to sensors, then tap to place, drag to move, and tap a mast to remove. Hovering a mast or the mast-height slider (tapping a mast on touch) previews what the masts see. Planned flights avoid terrain the sensors can see; exposed legs draw red, and a mast flashes red while it has a clear sight line to the drone. Sensors persist until the terrain changes or clear sensors.

Sharing. The whole scenario lives in the page URL — bookmark or send it and it restores exactly, re-launching a flight if A and B are set. A bare open rolls fresh terrain.

Verification. flat plane → a visible disk out to the curvature/refraction horizon √(2·Reff·h) ≈ 5.45 km at h = 2 m: raise max range to ≥9000 m and click near a corner (or drop observer height to ~0.5 m) so the horizon arc falls inside the 6×6 km map. single ridge → click west of the ridge for a clean shadow behind it; a low observer grazes the rounded crest, so bare ground at the top is hidden but a 2 m target standing on it is seen.
