help & verification tips Verification: flat plane → visible disk out to the curvature/refraction horizon √(2·Reff·h) ≈ 5.45 km at h = 2 m — raise max range to ≥9000 m, then click near a corner (or drop observer height to ~0.5 m) so the horizon arc falls inside the 6×6 km map. single ridge → click west of the ridge for a clean shadow behind it. Drone mode: click A then B; the drone flies straight at the set height above local ground, and the overlay follows it; the route plans around terrain the drone can't clear under max alt. Height/range edits apply live mid-flight. In either mode, drag on the map to pilot the observer/drone manually with live visibility. Exposure mode inverts the question: click a target and the green overlay shows every cell an observer (at observer height) could see it from.
